What "decoded" means. For each programme we price every dish on the fixed-price menu against the restaurant's own everyday à la carte menu, then say plainly whether the deal saves you money, which picks make it worth it, and where we had to estimate. Every number shows its source.

How the guides are made. Menus are captured from each programme's public listings; prices are verified on each restaurant's own published menu, with benchmarked estimates clearly marked and shown with their working. Corrections land in each guide's public commit history.
